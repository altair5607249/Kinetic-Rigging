# Doctor Octopus — Claw
This is my first kinetic rigging project!

## Context
After my sister bought tickets to the New York Comic Con for us, sister decided to cosplay as Doc Ock.

Like many of her previous costumes, it would be zany and elaborate. She took lots of inspiration from these video references:
- [Doctor Octopus Tentacles | Spider-Man 2 | No Way Home](https://youtu.be/ioZFJfpb1lU?is=K2dquD2wcrZ6yNLm) by Cardboard Man props & DIY
- [How to make Doctor Octopus Mechanical Arms (easy budget friendly)](https://youtu.be/xhopKo070xc?is=MYqT7gmNuxUYDjcz) by JamCrafts Ph

Because she wanted to go above and beyond, however, she wanted something more out of her costume, and that something would eventually come to mind as dynamic claws. Knowing I was good with this kind of stuff, she asked for my help, and knowing I know nothing about embedded software, I agreed to work with her (without telling her I was a noob).

Unfortunately, time constraints, schoolwork, and other projects ended up being too strict for me and my schedule, and I ended up not being able to rig the costume's claws on time. We still went to the convention, but we promised ourselves that we would finish the costume in the future.

It's been few months since my failure, and now I aim to rig at least one claw, and then eventually all of them (somehow)!
